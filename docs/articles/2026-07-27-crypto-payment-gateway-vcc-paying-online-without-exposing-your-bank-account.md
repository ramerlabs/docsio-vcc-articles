## Common mistakes to avoid when using USDT for virtual cards

- Sending USDT on the wrong network (e.g., ERC-20 instead of TRC-20) can cause lost funds. Always match the network supported by your VCC platform.
- Ignoring conversion fees. Some platforms charge up to 2-3% to convert USDT to USD. Factor this into your budget.
- Using a single card for all payments. If that card gets compromised, all subscriptions fail. Diversify into multiple cards.
- Forgetting to top up before auto-renewals. Ads or SaaS tools may fail if the card balance is insufficient. Set reminders.
