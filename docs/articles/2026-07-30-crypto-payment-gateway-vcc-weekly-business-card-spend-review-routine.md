## Step-by-step weekly review process

Follow these eight steps every Friday to keep your spend lean and intentional.

1. **Export all transactions from the past seven days.** Log into your virtual card dashboard and download a CSV. This gives you a raw list of every charge, including merchant name, amount, and date.
2. **Match each charge to a known vendor or subscription.** Go through the list line by line. Highlight any merchant you don't recognize or any amount that seems off.
3. **Check for duplicate or double charges.** Sometimes a vendor bills twice due to a glitch. Compare amounts and dates closely. If you see two identical charges, flag them.
4. **Review spend against your budget per category.** Compare actual ad spend against your planned ad budget. Do the same for SaaS, shipping, and other categories.
5. **Cancel or pause any unused subscriptions.** If you haven't used a tool all week, close its virtual card or reduce its limit. This prevents future charges while you decide whether to keep it.
6. **Adjust card limits for next week.** Increase limits for high-performing campaigns, and decrease or lock cards for underperformers. This proactive step prevents overspend.
7. **Document any refunds or disputes.** If you found a duplicate charge, initiate a refund request. Keep a log so you can follow up the next week.
8. **Archive the weekly report.** Save your CSV and notes in a shared folder. Over time, these reports reveal spending patterns that help you negotiate better vendor rates.

## Practical checklist for your weekly review

Use this checklist to ensure you never miss a critical step during your review.

- Exported all transactions from the last 7 days (include pending ones).
- Identified at least one unknown or suspicious charge.
- Checked for duplicate payments from the same merchant.
- Compared ad spend this week vs. the previous week (look for spikes).
- Paused or reduced limits on any card not used in 7 days.
- Logged any refunds or disputes in a tracking sheet.
- Updated budget allocations for the upcoming week.
- Reviewed card expiration dates to avoid service interruptions.

## Common mistakes to avoid

Even with a solid routine, business owners often slip into bad habits. Here are five mistakes that waste time and money.

- **Skipping the review because you're busy.** Missing one week leads to two, and soon you're back to monthly chaos. Schedule it as a recurring calendar block.
- **Using a single card for everything.** When all charges come from one card, you can't easily see which vendor caused a spike. Use separate [no kyc virtual credit cards](https://vccbusiness.com) for each major category.
- **Ignoring small recurring charges.** A $5 monthly fee for an unused plugin seems trivial, but ten such fees cost $600 a year. Small leaks sink budgets.
- **Not tracking refunds to completion.** If you dispute a charge, follow up weekly. Vendors often delay, and without a reminder, you may lose the refund window.
- **Failing to adjust limits after a campaign ends.** A card set to $500 for a one-week campaign will keep charging if you don't reduce the limit. Always lock cards after the campaign finishes.
